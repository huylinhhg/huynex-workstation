# CHUNG NHAN CAM KET BAO MAT & MINH BACH 100%
**HuyNex Zero-Latency Power-User Workstation**

Bo cai dat nay tuan thu cac tieu chuan ky thuat an toan cao nhat:

### 1. Khong cai dat bat ky tien trinh chay ngam nao (Zero-Daemon)
- **Tren macOS:** Khong tao LaunchAgent, khong tao LaunchDaemon, khong chay 24/7.
- **Tren Windows:** Khong tao Windows Service, khong tao Scheduled Task, khong ghi Registry Run ngam.
- Toan bo may khong bi ton du chi 1 MB RAM chay ngam.

### 2. Ma nguon mo 100% - Truc tiep kiem tra tung dong lenh
- Toan bo kich ban la file text thuan tuy (.command, .cmd, .ps1, .sh), ban co the mo bang Notepad hoac TextEdit de doc tung dong lenh truoc khi chay.
- Khong co bat ky file nhi phan (.exe, .pkg, .dmg) dong goi mo am nao.

### 3. Khong thu thap du lieu (Zero-Telemetry)
- Khong gui bat ky du lieu nao ve may chu ben ngoai.
- Chi ket noi internet de tai cac cong cu ma nguon mo tu nguon chinh thuc cua Apple (Homebrew) va Microsoft (winget).

### 4. Tu dong don dep sach se sau khi cai dat
- Sau khi hoan tat, ban chi can nhan Enter la thu muc cai dat tam nay se tu dong xoa chinh no khoi may de giu cho man hinh luon gon gang 100%.
